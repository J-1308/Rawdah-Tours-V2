# Photo slots

Places on the site waiting for a photo from outside Rawdah's own trips. Each one shows a dashed "Photo to come"
frame until a file with its name lands in this folder. Save the photo as `<slot name>.jpg` (or .png / .webp), push,
and the frame becomes the photo. No code change needed.

## Rules for outside photos

- **Licence:** free sites whose licence allows commercial use (Unsplash, Pexels), or paid stock (Adobe Stock, iStock).
  Keep the page link for each photo in the table below, so the licence can be checked later.
- **No faces you can recognise** in free stock: the licences don't cover the people in them. Backs, silhouettes,
  crowds far off and places are safe.
- **Never present stock people as Rawdah travellers.** Captions and alt text describe the scene, not "our group".
- **Never take photos from another Umrah operator's site.** Hotel and train photos come from Rawdah or the hotels'
  own media kits (those frames are already on the trip pages as "Photo to come").
- **Size:** at least 2000px on the long edge, landscape or portrait both fine. JPEG.
