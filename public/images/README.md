# Photos

One folder per kind of shot, so the photos in a folder compare like for like. Each file is the folder's name plus a
number, and **the number is the rank: 01 is the best photo in that folder** (Jibril's ranking, 31 October 2026).
The site uses the best ones first. Keep the pattern `folder-NN.jpg` (two digits) so the folders sort in order.

| Folder | What goes in it | Photos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `group-photos/` | The whole group posed for the camera, at a landmark | 7 |
| `friends/` | Two to six travellers posed for the camera | 11 |
| `candid/` | Natural moments, nobody looking at the camera | 8 |
| `teaching/` | Lessons, talks and guiding | 6 |
| `team/` | A team member facing the camera, Rawdah vest | 3 |
| `vest-back/` | The Rawdah vest from behind, logo showing | 4 |
| `bird-park/` | At the bird park, with the macaws | 5 |
| `places/` | Places with no one in focus | 1 |
| `portraits/` | One headshot per person, named after them (not ranked) | 4 |
| `brand/` | Logo and mark (not ranked) | 4 |
| `video/` | Video clips | 1 |

All photos are from Rawdah's own trips (Path of Purity II, August 2026), supplied by Jibril on 24 and 26 October 2026:
long edge 2400–3000px, JPEG, location data removed. Hotel and train photos still to come from Rawdah or the hotels'
media kits; never from another operator's site.

**Check before use:** "check age" means the photo may show under-18s and needs a parent's consent. candid-02 shows
sisters: check they are happy to be on the site. Photos Jibril chose not to use are in `photos-not-used/` at the
repo root (outside `public/`, so they are not deployed).
