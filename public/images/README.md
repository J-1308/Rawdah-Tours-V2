# Photos

One folder per kind of photo. Inside each folder the files are numbered, and **the number is the rank: 01 is
the best photo of that kind, the highest number the weakest.** Jibril ranks them; the site then uses the best
ones first. Keep the pattern `kind-NN.jpg` (two digits) so the folders sort in order.

| Folder | What goes in it | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `groups/` | Three or more travellers together, lessons, the whole group | `group-01` … `group-17` |
| `travellers/` | One or two travellers | `traveller-01` … `traveller-14` |
| `team/` | Rawdah's team at work, the Rawdah vest | `team-01` … `team-13` |
| `places/` | Places with no one in focus: the Haram, the cities, hotels, the train | `place-01` |
| `portraits/` | One headshot per person, named after them (not ranked) | 4 |
| `brand/` | Logo and mark (not ranked) | 4 |
| `video/` | Video clips | `video-01.mp4` |

All photos are from Rawdah's own trips (Path of Purity II, August 2026), supplied by Jibril on 24 and 26
October 2026: long edge 2400–3000px, JPEG, location data removed. Hotel and train photos still to come from
Rawdah or the hotels' media kits; never from another operator's site.

**Check before use:** "check age" means the photo may show under-18s and needs a parent's consent. group-03
shows sisters: check they are happy to be on the site.
